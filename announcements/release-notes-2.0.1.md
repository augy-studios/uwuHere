## Installing

- **Installed 2.0.0 on Android?** It can't open to update itself, so download `uwuhere-2.0.1.apk` on your phone and open it. It installs over 2.0.0, and your events and settings stay.
- **Still on 1.0.x on Android?** Tap **Update** on the bar at the top, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray, and it goes straight to 2.0.1. Your events come across by themselves.
- **Windows, macOS, AppImage**: uwuHere offers **Restart to update** by itself. For the `.msi`, `.deb` and `.rpm`, install the new file over the old one.
- New to uwuHere? The steps for each device are on the [2.0.0 release](https://github.com/augy-studios/uwuHere/releases/tag/android-v2.0.0).

## What's new

### Android app
- **Opens again**: 2.0.0 closed as soon as it started, on every phone. Making the app smaller for release took out a part the home screen widgets need, and nothing could start without it.
- **Notification switches stay on**: turning one on, then allowing notifications, no longer shows the switch off again. It was on all along.
- **One switch for new versions**: the Updates page and the Notifications window now always agree on **New versions of the app**.

### Windows, macOS and Linux apps
- **The title bar's buttons work**: Notifications, Report a bug, Show me around and Theme now open when clicked. In 2.0.0 they did nothing.
- **The search bar starts empty**: after you open something from it, it goes back to the page's name, and the next search starts from nothing.

### Everywhere
- **Skip stands out**: the tour's Skip button has its own background, so the app's name behind it no longer shows through.
