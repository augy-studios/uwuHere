The Windows, macOS and Linux apps can now wear your computer's own glass: Fluent 2 on Windows, Liquid Glass on macOS Tahoe, and Deepin's and COSMIC's frosted glass on Linux. This release is for computers only: the Android app stays at 2.0.1.

## Download

| Device | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 or 11 (x64 or Arm) | `uwuhere-2.0.2-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`, for installing on many computers at once) |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 and later) | `uwuhere-2.0.2-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `uwuhere-2.0.2-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Debian, Ubuntu, Mint (x64 or arm64) | `uwuhere-2.0.2-amd64.deb` or `uwuhere-2.0.2-arm64.deb` |

On Android, `uwuhere-2.0.1.apk` is on the [2.0.1 release](https://github.com/augy-studios/uwuHere/releases/tag/android-v2.0.1). On an iPhone or any other device, use [here.uwuapps.org](https://here.uwuapps.org) in the browser. Attendees never need an app: the website checks them in.

## Installing

- **Windows, macOS, AppImage**: uwuHere offers **Restart to update** by itself. For the `.msi`, `.deb` and `.rpm`, install the new file over the old one.
- New to uwuHere? The steps for each device are on the [2.0.0 release](https://github.com/augy-studios/uwuHere/releases/tag/android-v2.0.0).

## What's new

### Windows, macOS and Linux apps
- **Your system's glass**: under **Theme** > **Look**, the second choice is now your computer's own design, in your accent colour. It takes Material You's place, which suits a phone better than a desktop.
- **Windows: Fluent 2**: Mica behind the window, Acrylic glass on panels and dialogs, and Segoe UI Variable, as Windows 11's own apps have.
- **macOS: Liquid Glass**: see-through panels that catch the light at their edges, a floating glass sidebar, and capsule buttons, as macOS Tahoe has.
- **Linux: Frosted Glass**: blurred, see-through panels with round corners and a floating sidebar, as Deepin (DDE) and COSMIC have, with the window's background blurred where the desktop can do it. Deepin's and COSMIC's accent colours are read as well as GNOME's.
- **Easy to read on any glass**: writing and the edges of controls keep their contrast whatever your accent colour. Where the system can't show its glass (Windows 10, Wayland), soft glows in your accent colour stand in.
- **Chose Material You before?** The app opens in your system's glass. The UwU Apps look is still the first choice, and still the default.
