Two home screen widgets for Android: Your event and Check in.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwuhere-1.0.2.apk` |

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in
the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Play Protect
asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**.

Your events and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Android app
- **Your event widget**: how many people are at the event you're hosting, how many left early,
  and who checked in last, on your home screen. Tap it to open the event's host page. It shows
  the event you last opened in the app, and refreshes every 15 minutes until the event ends, even
  with the app closed, or straight away from its refresh button. The name of whoever checked in
  last shows on your home screen, so leave the widget off if others can see your phone.
- **Check in widget**: two buttons on your home screen, **Check in** to type an event's code and
  **Host** to make a new event.
- **Both widgets** follow your phone's light or dark mode, and fit themselves to the size you
  give them.

To add one, touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap **Widgets**, and look for
uwuHere.
