## Installing

- **Already have the Android app?** It updates itself: tap **Update** on the bar at the top, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. Your events, their host keys and your settings come across by themselves the first time 2.0.0 opens.
- **Android**: download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**.
- **Windows**: open the `.exe`. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time: click **More info** > **Run anyway**. It installs for you alone, no administrator needed.
- **Mac**: open the `.dmg` and drag uwuHere into Applications. The first time, right-click it and choose **Open**; on macOS 15 or later, go to **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. If it says the app is damaged, run `xattr -cr "/Applications/uwuHere.app"` in Terminal.
- **Linux**: `sudo apt install ./uwuhere-2.0.0-amd64.deb`, or `sudo dnf install ./uwuhere-2.0.0-x86_64.rpm`. For the AppImage, make it executable and open it.

## What's new

### Android app
- **A real app**: every screen built for Android, quick to open, and your events' lists there with no connection from the very first open.
- **Material You by default**: your phone's font and, on Android 12 and later, your wallpaper's colours. The UwU Apps look from the website, with its brand colours and the Jua font, is one switch away in **Theme**.
- **Updates page**: what's new in this version and every one before it, and **Update** downloads the next version, checks it and installs it, with a wavy line to show how far it's got.
- **Notifications**: check-ins to your events, a notification the moment a new version is out, and news, each its own switch under the bell.
- **Your events come with you**: everything the 1.x app kept, host keys included, is brought across the first time 2.0.0 opens.
- **Tap phones, Bluetooth, the hotspot and both home screen widgets** all work as before.
- **Back does what you'd expect**: a host or check-in page goes back home, and a second press sends the app to the background, so your event keeps taking check-ins.
- **No ads and no analytics** in the app.

### Windows, macOS and Linux apps
- **New**: uwuHere on your computer, for hosting from a laptop at the door. Same wifi and location check-ins, the big QR code, the CSV straight to Downloads.
- **Your events beside the page**: on a wide window your events and the code box stay on the left, and the event you pick opens on the right.
- **Its own title bar**, with back and forward through the pages and events you've opened, and a **command bar** (`Ctrl+K`): find an event by its name or code, type a code to check in, run any command, or paste a link.
- **Make it yours**: the first window picks the theme, the brand colour (or your computer's own accent colour) and whether uwuHere starts when you sign in.
- **Your event as a desktop widget**: how many are here and who came last, on your desktop, kept on top if you like.
- **Tray glance**: an event's count in the tray's tooltip (and beside the menu bar icon on a Mac), and your events in the Jump List and the Dock.
- **Check-in notifications** while uwuHere runs, even from the tray.
- **Screenshots**, including a long one of the whole page, copied and saved to Downloads.
- **Updates itself**: it checks as it opens and every hour, downloads the update and offers **Restart to update**.

### Everywhere
- **Report a bug**: say what went wrong, add up to three pictures or use **Screenshot a page**, which takes the whole page, top to bottom, and it reaches Augy straight away. You get a report ID to quote. Never an event's name, code or list, unless a picture shows them.
- **Shown around the first time**: a short tour of home, the host page and the check-in page, which scrolls to each thing it shows. **Show me around** in the Edge Tray replays them.
- **Privacy**: a page at [here.uwuapps.org/privacy](https://here.uwuapps.org/privacy) says what's kept, where it goes and when it's deleted.
- **A new icon**, centred and clear in any shape your launcher gives it.
