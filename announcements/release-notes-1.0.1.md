Tap phones to check in, Bluetooth check-in from app to app, your phone as the wifi when the venue has no internet, and the whole screen for the app.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwuhere-1.0.1.apk` |

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or Files. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**.

Your events and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Android app
- **Tap phones to check in**: turn on **Tap phones** for an event and keep it open in the app. People hold their phone against the back of yours, and their phone opens the check-in page, no app needed. Each tap works once, so a link passed on to someone who isn't there won't get them in. Needs NFC on your phone.
- **Bluetooth, app to app**: turn on **Bluetooth** for an event, and attendees who also have the uwuHere app check in phone to phone, with no internet needed on either phone.
  - Needs Google Play services on both phones.
  - Android asks for **Nearby devices** once, when you tap **Allow** on your event page.
  - Attendees in a browser don't see it, so keep another way on for them.
- **No internet at the venue?** Your phone can be the wifi. Tap **Start** next to "No internet for attendees?" on your event page, and show the two QR codes: one joins your phone's wifi, the other opens the check-in page.
  - Works with any phone, iPhones too, with no app.
  - Check-ins land in your list straight away, and reach uwuHere once you're back online.
  - Keep your event page open while it's on. Android 8.0 or newer.
- **The whole screen**: no status bar, so the page starts right at the top. Swipe down from the top to see the time and your notifications.

### Everywhere
- **Four ways to check in**: Same wifi, Location, Tap phones and Bluetooth, in **Create event** and **Event settings**.
- **Already on the check-in page?** In Chrome on Android, tap **Tap phones to check in**, then touch the host's phone: you're checked in on the page you're on. On other phones, the tap opens a new tab that checks you in with the name you've typed, and the first tab follows.
- **Tapped with no signal?** If you've opened uwuHere before, your tap is kept and sent as soon as you're back online, at the time you tapped. The host sees it as "Tapped, sent later".
- **uwuHere at the top is no longer a link**: the home button in the Edge Tray takes you home.
