The first Android app for uwuHere: scan a host's QR code to check in right in the app, a screen that stays on and a full-brightness QR code for hosts, the list saved to Downloads, and check-in notifications.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwuhere-1.0.0.apk` |

## Updating

**Android**: download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or Files. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**. From then on, the app updates itself: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray.

Your events and settings from the website don't carry over to the app, as each keeps its own. To manage an event you made on the website, enter its code under **Your events** and its password.

## What's new

### Android app
- **Scan to check in**: scan a host's QR code with your phone's camera, and it opens straight in the app, on the check-in button.
- **The host's screen stays on**: while **Same wifi** check-in is ready, the screen doesn't sleep, so nobody's check-in misses your phone. It sleeps as usual once you end the event or leave the page.
- **QR code at full brightness**: **Show the QR code big** turns the screen to full brightness, so the back of the room can scan it, and back again when you close it. Your phone's own brightness setting isn't changed.
- **The list saved to Downloads**: the **CSV** button saves the attendance list straight to your Downloads folder. On Android 9 or older, you pick where it goes.
- **Check-in notifications**: tap the bell in the Edge Tray and turn on **Check-ins to my events**. When someone checks in by location or leaves early, you hear about it, even with the app closed. Same-wifi check-ins don't notify, as they arrive on your open screen.
  - **New versions of the app** and **News from uwuHere** can be turned on there too.
  - Android asks to allow notifications only when you turn one on.
- **Check for updates**: the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray checks for a new version whenever you like. If you're up to date, it says so. If a new version is out, it tells you which one, with **Update** or **Not now**. Android only asks permission to install apps once you tap **Update**.
- **Location** goes through Android's own prompt, asked for only when you check in by location or set an event's spot.

### Everywhere
- **No ads**: uwuHere no longer shows ads, on the website or in the app.

Only checking in by location is ever sent from your phone with where you are, and only the distance from the host is kept.
