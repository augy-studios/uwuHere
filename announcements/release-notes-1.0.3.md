## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**.

Your events and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Android app
- **Tap to check in, in the app**: when you touch your phone to a host's phone that's taking taps, the event's join page now opens in uwuHere, not your browser. You still check in the same way. Phones without the app open the page in their browser, as before. Your phone needs NFC switched on.
- **An NFC app**: Android now lists uwuHere as an app that opens NFC tags, under **Launch via NFC** on phones that have that setting. It only opens uwuHere's own links. Any other tag goes to whatever app opened it before.
